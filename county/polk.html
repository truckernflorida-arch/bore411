Right-of-way permit cheat sheet

Office: Polk County Roads and Drainage reviews Utility Right of Way Permits and road and lane closures on county roads.

  • Lane closure request needed when working within 15 feet of the edge of a county road for more than two hours.
  • Submit closure requests at least 10 working days ahead, with a location map and a Temporary Traffic Control (TTC) plan. No fee for a closure request.
  • Utility permit fee: data being compiled.

Source: Polk County Roads and Drainage
