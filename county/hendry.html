Right-of-way permit cheat sheet

Permit: Utility Permit from the County Engineer (LaBelle), with a sketch or set of plans attached.

  • Underground crossings: at least 30 inches below pavement and 18 inches below the roadside ditch, unless staff approve an exception.
  • If jack and bore is permitted, the bore must extend at least 5 feet past the edge of pavement.
  • Inspection requests by email only, 24 hours before the work. Survey and mark the right-of-way lines before starting.
  • Disturbed right-of-way must be grassed or seeded after the work.
  • Fee: data being compiled.

Source: Hendry County Utilities Permit and conditions
