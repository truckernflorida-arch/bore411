Before you hire

Verify the license

Look up the license number and status in the DBPR license search. Confirm the company is active on Sunbiz. More on categories and experience paths on the licensing page.

Insurance and E-Verify

  • Ask for a certificate of insurance sent by the insurer, naming who the prime contract requires
  • E-Verify enrollment if the job is public (e-verify.gov)

811 is per excavator

Each excavator, including each sub, pulls its own Sunshine 811 ticket before digging (F.S. 556.105). Use the 811 dig-date calculator.

Need a short-term match?

For one-off sub work on a listed lead, use the sub board instead of a standing profile. Bore411 does not bid, take a cut, or share licenses.
