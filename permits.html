Right-of-way permit cheat sheets

Who issues the permit to bore in public right-of-way in the ten counties around Arcadia, and what their own pages say. Every item links to the official county or FDOT source. Checked November 1, 2026.

Informational only. Not legal advice. Rules, fees and forms change; confirm with the permitting office before you bid or start work. A right-of-way permit does not replace your Sunshine 811 ticket (F.S. 556) or any license the work requires.

First: who owns the road?

State road (FDOT)

All ten counties below are in FDOT District One. Utility work on state-road right-of-way is permitted by FDOT through One Stop Permitting (OSP), not by the county.

Sources: FDOT Utilities Office · FDOT District One utilities · District One county map.

County road or city street

County-maintained roads go through the county office listed below. Streets inside a city (for example Arcadia, Punta Gorda, Sebring, Wauchula, Fort Myers, Cape Coral) are usually permitted by the city, so check with the city when the job is inside city limits. One bore can cross more than one owner, which can mean more than one permit.

DeSoto County

Permit: Right-of-Way Permit from DeSoto County Engineering, for work that encroaches on county-maintained roads.

  • Fill in the Right-of-Way Permit Application (PDF) and email it to Engineering (address on the county forms page).
  • The form asks for the work address, crossroads, proposed work and, for new construction, the building permit number.
  • The applicant is responsible for utility locates (F.S. 556); traffic must be maintained per the MUTCD.
  • An approved permit is good for 6 months or until the project is done, whichever comes first; a 30-day extension can be requested.
  • Fee: shown as a blank on the form; ask Engineering.

Sources: DeSoto permit applications & forms · ROW permit application (PDF) · Engineering

Charlotte County

Permit: Right of Way Utility Permit, required for all utility installations, overhead or underground, in county right-of-way and easements (Ordinance 2021-009 and the Engineering Design & Construction Standards).

  • Applied for in Accela with the subcontractor worksheet, construction plans and MOT (traffic) plans; Public Works and Charlotte County Utilities review it.
  • Contractors must register with the county (licenses and insurance) to work in the right-of-way. A bond is required in advance for utility contractors (home services excepted).
  • Fees: $140 right-of-way review at application, plus an issuance fee set by Public Works by scope. The county reports it does not collect permit fees on fiber permits because of the Communications Services Tax.
  • Before boring: locates at least 48 hours ahead, written notice to county staff 48 hours before work, daily street sheets, and potholing to physically locate utilities.

Sources: ROW Utility Permit · ROW permit requirements · County presentation, May 19, 2026 (PDF)

Hardee County

Office: Hardee County Public Works (Wauchula).

  • We did not find a published right-of-way or utility permit form online. Contact Public Works before boring in county right-of-way.
  • Utility design and easement rules are in Section 5.10 of the Unified Land Development Code (adopted October 12, 2023).
  • Fee and timeline: data being compiled.

Sources: Public Works · Unified Land Development Code (PDF)

Highlands County

Permit: General Utility Permit from County Engineering, for all underground and overhead utility crossings on and along county roads and public right-of-way.

  • Apply online through eTRAKiT with the checklist items, including scaled installation drawings.
  • Fee: $400 for a General Utility Permit; unauthorized work in the right-of-way is $1,000 plus restoration (county fee schedule, checked Nov 1, 2026).

Sources: Utility Permits · Online submittal checklist (PDF) · Fee schedule · eTRAKiT

Sarasota County

Permit: Right-of-Way Use Permit (Public Works), applied for online in Accela under the Public Works module.

  • The county's Utility Accommodation Guide and ROW Use Procedures (2023) set out what goes in the application.
  • Work on county water and sewer systems also needs a separate Utility Construction Permit from Public Utilities, which asks for copies of any right-of-way use permits.
  • Fee: data being compiled.

Sources: Online permitting (Accela) · Utility Accommodation Guide & ROW Use Procedures (PDF) · Public Utilities permitting

Manatee County

Permit: Right-of-Way Use Permit, required before installing utilities, fiber, gas lines, culverts or other facilities in public right-of-way.

  • Telephone, electric, cable TV, gas and other private-sector operations must apply. The county says it processes about 400 of these permits a year.
  • Applied for through the county's online services portal.
  • Fee: data being compiled.

Source: Request a Right of Way Use Permit

Lee County

Office: Lee County DOT, Engineering Services, issues permits for construction in county right-of-way.

  • Use the DOT right-of-way application and the checklist for utility and communication providers.
  • Details and fees: data being compiled (Lee County's site blocked our automated check; open the links directly).

Sources: Lee DOT Engineering Services · ROW application (PDF) · Utility & communication provider checklist (PDF)

Glades County

Office: Glades County Road & Bridge Department (Moore Haven), which issues permits for utilities in county-owned right-of-way.

  • Road rules are in Chapter 58 (Roads and Bridges) of the county code.
  • Form, fee and timeline: data being compiled; contact Road & Bridge.

Sources: Road & Bridge Department · Code Chapter 58

Hendry County

Permit: Utility Permit from the County Engineer (LaBelle), with a sketch or set of plans attached.

  • Underground crossings: at least 30 inches below pavement and 18 inches below the roadside ditch, unless staff approve an exception.
  • If jack and bore is permitted, the bore must extend at least 5 feet past the edge of pavement.
  • Inspection requests by email only, 24 hours before the work. Survey and mark the right-of-way lines before starting.
  • Disturbed right-of-way must be grassed or seeded after the work.
  • Fee: data being compiled.

Source: Hendry County Utilities Permit and conditions

Polk County

Office: Polk County Roads and Drainage reviews Utility Right of Way Permits and road and lane closures on county roads.

  • Lane closure request needed when working within 15 feet of the edge of a county road for more than two hours.
  • Submit closure requests at least 10 working days ahead, with a location map and a Temporary Traffic Control (TTC) plan. No fee for a closure request.
  • Utility permit fee: data being compiled.

Source: Polk County Roads and Drainage
