HDD bore calculators

Estimates only. These are field rules of thumb and published industry tables, not an engineered bore plan. Follow the conduit maker's limits, your fluid supplier's mix sheet and the project engineer's specs.

1. Backreamer size

Ream to at least 1.5 times the outside diameter of the conduit or bundle (PPI TN-63, section 4.3).

2. Drilling fluid volume

Hole volume in gallons per foot = diameter² ÷ 24.5, then times a soil factor.

3. Safe pull strength for HDPE conduit

The most tension a single HDPE conduit should see while it is pulled in, from PPI TN-63 (2021) Tables 3–5, adjusted for time under tension and conduit temperature. Most drills can pull far more than this, so use a breakaway link sized for the conduit.

Formulas and sources

  • Reamer size. "The diameter of the bore hole that the conduit is being pulled into should be a minimum of 1.5 times the outside diameter of the conduit or conduit bundle" (PPI TN-63, 4.3.2). Conduit ODs are standard IPS outside diameters. Bundle OD for 2–7 equal conduits uses the smallest circle that fits them (2, 2.155, 2.414, 2.701, 3 and 3 times one conduit OD); real bundles ride looser, so treat it as a floor.
  • Fluid volume. Diameter² ÷ 24.5 = gallons per linear foot. Plan at least 2× hole volume in sand or dirt-like soil and 3–5× in clay and reactive soils (The Driller, "Bore Planning"; The Driller, "Calculating Fluid Volumes, Pressures for Horizontal Drilling," 2018). Run each pass (pilot, pre-ream, final ream) separately and add them up.
  • Minutes per rod. Gallons per rod (gal/ft × factor × rod length) ÷ pump gpm, from The Driller, "Tips for Estimating Rod Pullback Time in HDD". Thicker fluid lowers the real pump rate.
  • Safe pull strength. SPS = π · D² · FY · Ft · FT · (1/DR − 1/DR²), with FY 1,200 psi (standard) or 1,400 psi (higher strength), the 40% of yield limit. Table values at 73 °F and 1 hour or less, times Ft (0.95 for over 1–12 h, 0.91 for over 12–24 h) and FT (Table A1). PPI TN-63, Safe Pull Strength Calculations for Conduit (2021). TN-63 also advises 3–5% extra slack and letting the conduit relax at least as long as the pull took before cutting or terminating. For full mini-HDD wall checks, use PPI's Conduit Design Calculator.
  • Bundle pull limits and steel or PVC pipe are not covered here. Ask the conduit or pipe supplier.
