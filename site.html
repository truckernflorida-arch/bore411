Job site check

Screen any Florida job site before you price it: a private job, a sub job, or a bid that is not on the board. Type a town, a county or the job-site coordinates (or use your phone's location) and every lookup from the bid pages runs for that point, plus the county, city limits and FDOT market area. Every lookup goes from your browser straight to the free public data services named on each card.

Where is the job?

Town and county points are U.S. Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer internal points, not a job site. For the exact site, paste coordinates (from Google Maps: long-press the spot, then copy the numbers) or tap "Use my location" while standing on it. "Use my location" only fills in the coordinates on this page.

Soil screening (USDA)

Screening-level, not a geotechnical report. Mapped soil at the point, from USDA NRCS SSURGO via Soil Data Access. Soils change across a bore path; get borings for design.

Pick a point above.

Full maps: Web Soil Survey.

Sinkhole screening (FGS)

Screening-level, not a geotechnical report. Reported subsidence incidents near the point, from the Florida Geological Survey database (FDEP open data). Most reports were never field-checked; no reports nearby does not mean no sinkhole risk.

Pick a point above.

Source: FDEP / Florida Geological Survey, Subsidence Incident Reports · FGS sinkhole info. Owner and reporter details are not shown.

Geology & rock (FGS)

Screening-level, not a geotechnical report. Mapped geologic unit and lithology, near-surface rock or sediment, sinkhole type area and sinkhole favorability at the point, plus nearby Florida Geological Survey wells with logs on file. Flags mapped limestone or dolostone that could mean rock tooling.

Pick a point above.

Flood zone & wetlands (FEMA / USFWS)

Screening-level, not a flood zone determination or wetland delineation. FEMA flood zone, FIRM panel and mapped wetlands or water within 1,000 ft of the point. Flags pits, spoil and staging that may need a floodplain or environmental permit.

Pick a point above.

Contamination & cleanup sites (FDEP)

Screening-level, not an environmental site assessment. Open, active or on-hold FDEP cleanup sites (leaking petroleum tanks, drycleaners, PFAS, brownfields, Superfund) within 1/2 mile of the point. Drilling through contaminated ground can mean testing and hauling your returns and spoil, so ask before you price it.

Pick a point above.

Buried & overhead hazards (electric & pipelines)

Screening-level, not a locate. Mapped high-voltage electric transmission lines and major gas, petroleum, HGL and crude pipelines within 1/2 mile of the point. Distribution lines and service lines are not shown. Always call Sunshine 811.

Pick a point above.

Railroads & rail crossings (FRA)

Screening-level, not a survey or a railroad permit. Mapped rail lines (owner, track type) and FRA-inventoried rail crossings within 1 mile of the point, plus CSX's published utility crossing rules when a CSX line is close. A bore under or along tracks needs the railroad's own agreement, fees and insurance, which can set your start date.

Pick a point above.

Weather & rain-out check (NWS)

7-day forecast and any active alerts at the point, from the National Weather Service. Rain, lightning and wind drive mud handling, frac-out cleanup, locating and crew safety; use it to plan mobilization, not as a go/no-go.

Pick a point above.

Source: National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov), queried live. Forecasts change; check weather.gov the day before you mobilize.

Road owner & traffic (FDOT)

Screening-level, not a right-of-way survey. Roads within 1,000 ft of the point from FDOT's road inventory: on or off the State Highway System (FDOT permit or county/city permit), route numbers, traffic count, lanes and speed limit for traffic control planning.
