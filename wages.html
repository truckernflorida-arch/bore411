Davis-Bacon wage desk: HDD & underground

When a public job is paid with federal money, the bid package carries a U.S. Department of Labor wage determination (WD): the lowest base wage plus fringe you may pay each trade on site. This page shows the current Heavy and Highway WDs for the 10 counties around Arcadia, flags which ones have an HDD operator and locator rate, checks each rate against Florida's minimum wage, and works out a crew's daily wage floor for your bid. Informational only — not legal advice.

The WD in your bid documents governs. Use the wage determination number and modification printed in the solicitation; a revised WD issued at least 10 calendar days before bid opening applies to a sealed bid (29 CFR 1.6(c)(2)(ii)(A)). The contracting agency picks the construction type (Heavy or Highway); it is printed on the WD. Rates here were copied from SAM.gov on November 16, 2026.

Floor per hour = the higher of the WD total (base + fringe; fringe may be paid as cash or bona fide benefits, 29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)) and Florida's $15.00 minimum. Hours over 40 in a week add half the cash base rate (overtime at 1.5× the basic rate, 29 CFR 5.5(b)(1); Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards Act, contracts over $100,000). Wages only: payroll taxes, workers' comp, insurance, per diem and your margin are not included, so add your burden in the bid sheet.

3. How Davis-Bacon works on a bid

When it applies

Federal construction contracts over $2,000 must state the minimum wages for each class of laborers and mechanics (40 U.S.C. §3142(a)). Many federally assisted local jobs carry the same clauses under related acts. The solicitation will say so and attach the WD; if it doesn't, ask the agency before you bid.

No HDD class on the WD?

Most of these county WDs list no horizontal directional drill operator or locator. A class you need that isn't listed is added after award by conformance: the contracting officer requests it and the rate must bear a reasonable relationship to the WD (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)). Ask in your pre-bid questions which rate the agency expects for the drill operator and locator.

Subs are covered too

The prime must put the wage clauses and WD into every subcontract and is responsible for the subs' compliance (29 CFR 5.5(a)(6)). If you sub the bore, expect the prime to ask for your payrolls.

Weekly certified payroll

Each contractor and sub submits certified payrolls weekly for every week covered work is done; DOL's optional form is WH-347 (29 CFR 5.5(a)(3)(ii)). Keep payroll records at least 3 years after the prime contract is done (29 CFR 5.5(a)(3)(i)).
