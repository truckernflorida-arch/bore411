How it works

1. Post it like a load

Footage, conduit size & count, soil or rock, rate, and a map pin.

2. Crews see it fast

Alerts by distance and rig size go to crews near the job.

3. Crews respond

Rig, locator, area and availability, so you can pick the right fit.

4. Plus every public lead

Public bids and sub calls from other boards, linked to the original source.

One page per bid. Not ten websites.

Every lead gets its own bid package: due date with a calendar file, the original agency posting, your 811 call-by date, and a go/no-go checklist (Sunbiz, E-Verify, COI, bond, pre-bid, license class).
