Before you sign a sub deal

The sub needs its own license

Underground utility and excavation work is a specialty category, so a sub doing it needs its own certification or registration, not the prime's (F.S. 489.113(2)). Letting someone work under your license is a disciplinary violation (F.S. 489.129(1)(e)). More on the licensing page.

Check each other

  • License category and status in the DBPR license search
  • Company active on Sunbiz
  • Certificate of insurance sent by the insurer, naming who the prime contract requires
  • E-Verify enrollment if the job is public (e-verify.gov)

811 is per excavator

Each excavator, including each sub, pulls its own Sunshine 811 ticket before digging (F.S. 556.105). Use the 811 dig-date calculator to plan the start.

Price the shot

Compare per-foot numbers with FDOT price benchmarks, size the reamer and mud with the bore calculators, and check the county's ROW permit rules.
