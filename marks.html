Marks, tolerance zone & cover depths

What the paint means on a Florida job, how wide the caution strip is, and the FDOT minimum cover and HDD depth rules that often show up on District One permits. Built for crews around Arcadia. Informational only — not a locate, not a permit, not legal advice.

Before you dig: pull your own Sunshine 811 ticket, wait the full business days, check every positive response, and expose lines carefully inside the tolerance zone. Private lines are not marked by 811. County ROW rules still apply — see ROW permits.

Tolerance zone (Florida dig law)

Sunshine 811: underground facilities have a tolerance zone that runs 24 inches from the facility’s outside edges. Use increased precaution inside that strip. Dig only in the area described on your ticket.

How wide is the caution strip?

Use the pipe or conduit OD you expect, or leave at 0 if you only know the paint line.

24″OD24″

Total caution width = OD + 24″ + 24″. Soft-dig / hand-expose inside the zone; do not bucket through paint.

Ticket rules that trip crews

  • Notify Sunshine 811 not less than 2 full business days before digging (10 under waters of the state) — F.S. 556.105.
  • Ticket valid 30 calendar days (call day not counted).
  • Each excavator, including each sub, should pull its own ticket (Sunshine 811 FAQ; Chapter 556 covers directional drilling and boring).
  • Member utilities mark only what they own — private lines are not located by 811.
  • If marks are destroyed, stop there and request a refresh.

FDOT minimum cover (UAM 2017)

On FDOT right-of-way, Rule 14-46.001, F.A.C. incorporates the Utility Accommodation Manual, 2017 edition. Cover is measured to the top of the utility. HDD under pavement often needs more than the open-trench minimum — see the calculator below. Always confirm the permit and current UAM; county / city streets may set different depths (Hendry publishes crossing depths on our ROW sheet).

Roadway pavement

36″

Minimum below the top of roadway pavement (UAM §3.16.4(1)).

Unpaved / other pavement

30″

Minimum below existing unpaved ground or non-roadway pavement, including designed ditch grade (UAM §3.16.4(2)).

Limited-access crossing

48″

Minimum cover from pavement surface to top of utility for underground crossings of limited-access R/W; no open cut (UAM §4.3.3).

FDOT HDD depth & max bore (UAM §3.16.9.1)

Under roadway pavement, FDOT sets bore depth from top of pavement to top of the bore at 10 × bored diameter (or greater), unless you qualify for the water-table / confining-layer reduction in the UAM. Max bored diameter is capped by product (or casing) diameter. Clearance from existing vitrified clay sanitary or gas lines: at least 3.5 × bored diameter (UAM should-language).
