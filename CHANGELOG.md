## 2026-11-13
- Bid pages: new "Weather & rain-out check (NWS)" card. At the point picked in the soil card (town/county center or typed job-site coordinates) it queries the National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov, free, no key) live in the browser and shows any active NWS alerts for that point, the 7-day forecast by period (forecast, temperature, chance of rain, wind), and a one-line summary of daytime periods with a 50%+ chance of rain and any periods with thunderstorms. Shows the NWS forecast office and issue time (ET). The 50% "likely rain-out" cutoff is a Bore411 shortcut, labeled as such; for planning mobilization, not a go/no-go. No new page, so `sitemap.xml` is unchanged.

## 2026-11-12
- Bore calculators (`calc.html#mix`): new card 7, "Mud mix: bags per tank". Enter the mix tank size, pick a product and ground (BORE-GEL 25–35 lb/100 gal normal boring or 35–60 in poorly consolidated sand/gravel; QUIK-GEL GOLD 15–25 normal or 25–40 unconsolidated, all from Baroid IDP data sheets) or type any product's rate, set the bag size and soda ash (1–2 lb per 100 gal of make-up water per the Baroid Soda Ash sheet). It shows pounds and bags per tank (with lb/bbl), soda ash per tank, and, with a total fluid figure or the "Use card 2 total" button, tanks, bags and soda ash for the whole bore. Flags a typed rate outside the data-sheet range; notes to test water (pH 8.5–9.5) and add soda ash before bentonite. Sources and the Nov 12, 2026 check date added to "Formulas and sources". Labeled an estimate; supplier mix sheet and field tests govern. No brand endorsement.
- Fix: `scripts/gen_counties.py` did not write the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon, so rerunning it would have dropped analytics from the 11 county pages. It now adds it (regenerated pages are unchanged).

## 2026-11-11
- Bore calculators (`calc.html#log`): new card 6, "Rod-by-rod bore log (as-built record)". Enter the entry pitch and rod length, then the locator's pitch at the end of each rod (plus its depth reading and a note if you want). It works out how far out and how deep below entry grade the head is after every rod with the average angle method (each rod uses the mean of its start and end pitch angles; ISCWSA "Introduction to Wellbore Positioning" 7.1, checked Nov 11, 2026), shows the deepest point, and highlights any rod where the locator depth and the calculated depth differ by a foot or more. Works with locators that show downhill pitch as minus or plus. The log stays in the browser (localStorage, not sent to Bore411) until cleared; Undo last rod, Copy log (tab-separated, pastes into a spreadsheet) and Download CSV. Labeled an estimate and a field record, not a surveyed as-built; left/right steering is not tracked.

## 2026-11-10
- Price benchmarks (`benchmarks.html#bidsheet`): new "Bid sheet: your cost per foot vs. FDOT". Type your own daily labor, rig, support equipment and fuel/mud costs, feet of finished bore per day, material per foot, footage, one-time mobilization/permit/MOT/restoration costs and overhead + profit %. It shows your cost per foot, price per foot, crew days, job price and all-in price per foot, and how far that is above or below the FDOT 0630 2 12 directional-bore average for Area 09, 10, 08 or statewide (thin areas flagged; FDOT's item includes conduit, so subs usually sit below it). A quick takeoff prices typed quantities of the 8 FDOT items on the page (bore, trench and aboveground conduit, 2-12 fiber, four pull box sizes) at that area's average, falling back to statewide where the area had no awards, and a "Copy bid sheet" button copies everything as plain text. All prices are the published FDOT figures already on the page (lettings 2025/10/01 to 2026/09/30, run 10/26/2026); no costs are filled in for you and nothing is sent anywhere. Labeled an estimate. Linked from the Permit route & price benchmarks card on every bid page.

## 2026-11-08
- Bore calculators (`calc.html`): new card 5, "Bore path planner: setback to reach depth". Enter entry pitch (%), the depth where the bore should level out, the rod maker's minimum bend radius and rod length (presets: Ditch Witch JT2020 rod, 114 ft / 10 ft, and Vermeer D100x120 Firestick rod, 207 ft / 20 ft, from Premier Drill Products spec sheets) and a planning factor (default 1.5× the rod minimum, because those limits are for pure bending only; the factor is a Bore411 choice, labeled as such). It shows the horizontal setback from entry to level, the straight and bend segments, rods needed, and degrees / % pitch change per rod; if the bend can't level out that shallow it gives the steepest entry pitch that can. Optional pipe check against the HDPE cold bend minimum (20/25/27× OD by DR, PPI TN-27 Q. 60) and the 40× OD ovaling limit noted in PPI Handbook Ch. 12, using bundle OD for several conduits. Formulas and sources added; checked Nov 8, 2026. Estimate only.

## 2026-11-07
- Bore calculators (`calc.html`): new card 4, "Pullback force estimate (mini-HDD)". Pick IPS size, SDR 9–17, number of conduits, horizontal pull length, planned 90° bends and drill rod diameter; it estimates the pull on the conduit at the end of the pull with the PPI Handbook of PE Pipe, Chapter 12 (revised May 2026) mini-HDD method, Eqs. 22–25: F = w_b × L/3 × 1.6^n, w_b = OD²/2 − pipe weight, unplanned path corrections = 2L/(500 × rod diameter). Pipe weight uses minimum wall and 0.947 g/cm³ (low end of the ASTM D3350 PE4710 density cell). It compares the result per conduit with the TN-63 1-hour safe pull already on the page and flags when the estimate is over the standard-strength limit. Formula, source and Nov 7, 2026 check date added to "Formulas and sources". Labeled an estimate; not for maxi-HDD (ASTM F1962).
