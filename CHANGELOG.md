# Bore411 changelog

## 2026-11-08
- Bore calculators (`calc.html`): new card 5, "Bore path planner: setback to reach depth". Enter entry pitch (%), the depth where the bore should level out, the rod maker's minimum bend radius and rod length (presets: Ditch Witch JT2020 rod, 114 ft / 10 ft, and Vermeer D100x120 Firestick rod, 207 ft / 20 ft, from Premier Drill Products spec sheets) and a planning factor (default 1.5× the rod minimum, because those limits are for pure bending only; the factor is a Bore411 choice, labeled as such). It shows the horizontal setback from entry to level, the straight and bend segments, rods needed, and degrees / % pitch change per rod; if the bend can't level out that shallow it gives the steepest entry pitch that can. Optional pipe check against the HDPE cold bend minimum (20/25/27× OD by DR, PPI TN-27 Q. 60) and the 40× OD ovaling limit noted in PPI Handbook Ch. 12, using bundle OD for several conduits. Formulas and sources added; checked Nov 8, 2026. Estimate only.

## 2026-11-07
- Bore calculators (`calc.html`): new card 4, "Pullback force estimate (mini-HDD)". Pick IPS size, SDR 9–17, number of conduits, horizontal pull length, planned 90° bends and drill rod diameter; it estimates the pull on the conduit at the end of the pull with the PPI Handbook of PE Pipe, Chapter 12 (revised May 2026) mini-HDD method, Eqs. 22–25: F = w_b × L/3 × 1.6^n, w_b = OD²/2 − pipe weight, unplanned path corrections = 2L/(500 × rod diameter). Pipe weight uses minimum wall and 0.947 g/cm³ (low end of the ASTM D3350 PE4710 density cell). It compares the result per conduit with the TN-63 1-hour safe pull already on the page and flags when the estimate is over the standard-strength limit. Formula, source and Nov 7, 2026 check date added to "Formulas and sources". Labeled an estimate; not for maxi-HDD (ASTM F1962).

Sources and the Nov 6, 2026 check date are on the page. Linked from the 811 calculator, home desk checklist, and bid-package 811 card. Informational only — not a locate or a permit.

## 2026-11-05
- New `crews.html` "Crew directory" plus empty `crews.json` (hand-reviewed; no invented companies). Free public profiles for licensed Florida HDD and underground crews near Arcadia: company, DBPR license # (with link to the DBPR search so the user verifies), license type, counties, rig class, services, website and about. Phone and email are never published. County and rig filters; `?id=<slug>` detail view with a "Message this crew" Formspree form (`form_type=crew_message`, job-post endpoint). "List your crew" form (`form_type=crew_profile`, `_subject=Bore411 crew profile`) collects contact for review only. Intro cites F.S. 489.129(1)(e); "Before you hire" covers DBPR, Sunbiz, COI, E-Verify and per-excavator 811 (556.105). Bore411 does not bid, take a cut, or share licenses. Added to nav (before Equipment) on every page, home desk buttons, bid-page sub card, sitemap, and regenerated county pages.

## 2026-11-04
- New `subs.html` "Sub board" for prime-to-sub matching. Primes post work they need subbed and crews post when and where they can start, through two labeled forms (job-post Formspree, `form_type=sub_needed` / `sub_available`) with a picker of today's open leads, counties around Arcadia, rig class, start date, license needed, and the sub's DBPR license number. Open leads are listed with "Need a sub" and "Available" buttons that preselect the lead. A "before you sign" section covers the sub's own license (F.S. 489.113(2)), license lending (489.129(1)(e)), DBPR/Sunbiz/COI/E-Verify checks, and per-excavator 811 tickets (556.105). Bore411 does not broker or take a cut. Added to the sitemap.
- Bid pages: the "Need a sub? Available to sub?" buttons now open the sub board with that lead already picked (they used to go to the generic home form, which had no way to say which side you were on).
- Accessibility: home-page alert and post-a-bore fields have accessible names (they had placeholders only), every page shows a visible keyboard focus ring, and motion is reduced for users who ask for it.

## 2026-11-03
- New `licenses.html` "Licensing" page, linked in the nav on every page: a Florida underground utility and excavation contractor (CU) license checker. Enter years as skilled worker and foreman, college credit and degree, and it shows which of the five experience paths in F.S. 489.111(2)(c) you meet (degree in Building Construction, Civil or Mechanical Engineering per Rule 61G4-15.001(4)(e); experience must be under a properly licensed contractor and not exempt work, 61G4-15.001(1)). Credit check per Rule 61G4-15.006 (no unsatisfied judgments or liens; FICO 660 or the 14-hour course), insurance minimums $100,000 liability / $25,000 property damage (Rule 61G4-15.003), and the exam route (Professional Testing, Inc. approval, Pearson VUE scheduling, per DBPR). Also a table of what the license covers for HDD crews (489.105(3)(n), including the engineered empty-conduit rule), the telecom electrical exemption (489.503(14)), sub rules (489.113(2)), unlicensed contracts (489.128), license lending penalties (489.129), CU vs RU (489.117), and the DBPR license search. Informational only; fee amounts marked "data being compiled". Sources checked Nov 3, 2026.

## 2026-10-30
- Bid pages: the "Sinkhole screening" card is now live (was "coming soon"). It uses the point picked in the soil card (town/county center or job-site coordinates) and queries the Florida Geological Survey Subsidence Incident Reports database (FDEP open data) live in the browser: reported incidents within 1, 3, 5 and 10 miles, how many are field-verified sinkholes, and the 5 nearest reports with FGS reference number, distance, reported date, verified status and size. Shows the statewide report count and newest event date from the live database. Labeled screening-level, not a geotechnical report; notes that most reports are not field-checked and that no reports is not a clearance. Owner and reporter details are not requested or shown.
