# Bore411 — bore411.com

Free load board and Florida HDD bid desk for underground work (HDD, boring, trenching). Static site on GitHub Pages, no build step.

- `index.html` — home: hero, how it works, daily leads (from `leads.json`), alert + post-a-bore forms (Formspree).
- Home lead feed has a "Near" filter: type a Florida town or county (list from `fl-geo.json`), pick a radius (25/50/100/150 mi or any) and sort by due date or nearest. Distance is straight-line miles from that town center to the nearest place `geoCandidates()` finds in the lead's `location`; leads with no mappable place (statewide, area not stated) stay in the list, labeled. A star on each lead (and a Save button on bid pages) saves it; the "Saved" button shows only saved open leads. Town, radius, sort and saved leads are kept in the browser's localStorage (`b411.near`, `b411.saved`) only, never sent anywhere.
- `bid.html?id=…` — bid package for each lead, generated in the browser from `leads.json`. The id is derived from existing fields (bid # or title + agency), so the daily routine needs no new field.
- Bid pages include a USDA soil screening card: the lead's `location` text is matched to a town or county point in `fl-geo.json` (U.S. Census 2024 Gazetteer internal points), then the browser queries USDA NRCS Soil Data Access (SSURGO) live for map unit, major soils, drainage class, hydrologic group, water table, bedrock depth and flooding. Users can enter job-site coordinates instead. Screening-level only. Logic in `geoCandidates()` / `soilAt()` in `common.js`.
- Bid pages also include a sinkhole screening card: for the same point, the browser queries the Florida Geological Survey Subsidence Incident Reports layer (FDEP open data ArcGIS REST, `OpenData/FGS_SUBSIDENCE/MapServer/0`) for reports within 10 miles and shows counts at 1/3/5/10 mi, field-verified count, and the 5 nearest reports (ref #, distance, date, verified, size). Owner/reporter fields are never requested. Screening-level only. Logic in `sinkAt()` in `common.js`.
- `tools.html` — Florida 811 dig-date calculator (F.S. 556.105; Sunshine 811 holidays). Logic in `common.js`.
- `marks.html` — dig marks & cover desk: APWA Uniform Color Codes as used by Sunshine 811, Florida 24-inch tolerance-zone width calculator, and FDOT Utility Accommodation Manual (2017) minimum cover (36″ / 30″ / 48″ limited-access) plus HDD max bore diameter and 10× depth-under-pavement calculators (UAM §§3.16.4, 3.16.9.1, 4.3.3; Rule 14-46.001, F.A.C.). Every figure cited with source and check date. Linked in the nav on every page.
- `calc.html` — HDD bore calculators: backreamer size (1.5× conduit/bundle OD, PPI TN-63), drilling fluid volume (D²÷24.5 gal/ft times a soil factor, The Driller) with minutes per rod, HDPE conduit safe pull strength (PPI TN-63 Tables 3–5 with time and temperature factors), and a mini-HDD pullback force estimate (PPI Handbook of PE Pipe Ch. 12, Eqs. 22–25, revised May 2026) compared with that safe pull, and a bore path setback planner (entry pitch + bend radius to level out at a depth; rod presets from Premier Drill Products spec sheets; HDPE bend check per PPI TN-27 and Ch. 12), plus a rod-by-rod bore log (pitch per rod → distance out and depth below entry grade by the average angle method, ISCWSA; optional locator depth comparison; saved in localStorage, copy/CSV export). Self-contained, no external calls.
- `permits.html` — right-of-way permit cheat sheets for DeSoto, Charlotte, Hardee, Highlands, Sarasota, Manatee, Lee, Glades, Hendry and Polk counties plus FDOT state roads (One Stop Permitting), each item linked to the official source and dated. Bid pages link the matching county (`permitRoute()` in `bid.html`, matched from county and town names in the lead text) and flag postings that mention FDOT or a state/U.S. route.
- County GIS map links (official county/Property Appraiser/FDOT viewers) live in one object, `GIS` in `common.js` (`gisHtml()` renders it). `permits.html#gis` lists all 11; bid pages show a "County GIS map" card using the same county match as `permitRoute()`. Reference only; 811 tickets and field verification (F.S. 556) govern.
- `counties.html` and `county/<name>.html` — SEO landing pages for the 10 counties around Arcadia. Each shows open leads that name the county (same county/town regex as `permitRoute()` in `bid.html`) plus other open leads within 50 miles of the county center (lead location matched with `geoCandidates()`), a static copy of that county's ROW permit card from `permits.html`, its GIS links from `GIS`, the FDOT market area, and a live USDA soil and FGS sinkhole screen at the county center or main towns. Live parts are in `county.js` (reads `window.CTY`). The pages are generated by `scripts/gen_counties.py` (Python 3 + Node, no dependencies) from `permits.html`, `GIS` and `fl-geo.json`: after editing a county permit card or GIS link, rerun it.
- `licenses.html` — Florida underground utility and excavation contractor license checker (informational): experience paths from F.S. 489.111(2)(c) and Rule 61G4-15.001, credit/insurance minimums (Rules 61G4-15.006 and 61G4-15.003), exam route, a scope table for HDD/conduit work (F.S. 489.105(3)(n), 489.113, 489.103(5), 489.503(14)), sub and license-lending rules, and the DBPR license search link. Self-contained JS, no external calls. Linked in the nav on every page (county pages pick it up from `permits.html` via `scripts/gen_counties.py`).
- `benchmarks.html` — FDOT statewide 12-month item average prices plus Southwest Florida market areas 09, 10 and 08 with a county-to-market-area lookup (published figures only). Its `#bidsheet` section is a bid sheet: the user's own daily crew cost, feet per day, material and markup give cost and price per foot and a job price, compared with the FDOT 0630 2 12 directional-bore average for the picked area (`?area=a09|a10|a08|sw` preselects); plus a quick takeoff that prices typed quantities of the 8 listed FDOT items at that area's average (statewide where the area had no awards, thin items flagged) and a copy-as-text button. No default costs are filled in; inputs never leave the browser. Prices live in the `I` array in the page script, copied from the tables above, so update both together.
- `rigs.html` — HDD rig size classes with sourced specs, a rough rig picker, support-equipment checklist.
- `subs.html` — prime-to-sub board: two labeled forms ("I need a sub" / "I'm available to sub") posting to the job-post Formspree with `form_type=sub_needed` or `sub_available`, a lead picker filled from open leads in `leads.json` (sends `lead`, `lead_title`, `lead_url`), counties, rig class and dates. `?lead=<leadId>&as=av` preselects a lead and tab; bid pages link here. Also lists open leads with Need a sub / Available buttons and a sourced "before you sign" card (489.113(2), 489.129(1)(e), 556.105). Posts are reviewed by hand; no public listings yet.
- `crews.html` + `crews.json` — hand-reviewed Florida HDD / underground crew directory near Arcadia. Public profiles show company, license #, license type, counties, rig, services, website and about only — never phone or email. `crews.json` ships as `[]` until profiles are approved; each entry is `{id, name, license, license_type, counties, rig, services, website, about, updated}`. List-your-crew and message-this-crew forms post to the job-post Formspree (`form_type=crew_profile` / `crew_message`). Linked in the nav on every page (county pages via `scripts/gen_counties.py`).
- `equipment.html` — free equipment for-sale/rent board (starter); form posts to the job-post Formspree with `form_type=equipment`.
- Rig-class tags (est.) on leads come from `rigClass()` in `common.js`, parsed from the scope text; no extra leads.json field.
- `leads.json` — rewritten daily: `type, title, agency, location, bid, due` (or `open_until`), `scope, source, url`.

Changes are logged in `CHANGELOG.md`.

Images in `/images` are illustrative AI-generated pictures, not real Bore411 jobs or crews. The home hero (`hero-hdd-rig-v3.jpg`) is modeled on a real tracked HDD rig: inclined rack, rod entering the ground at an angle at the front, nothing out the back.
Bore411 does not bid work, take a percentage of any job, or hold or share contractor licenses. Public information only.
