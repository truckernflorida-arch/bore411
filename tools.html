The rules used

  • Notify Sunshine 811 not less than 2 full business days before excavation; 10 full business days for work beneath the waters of the state (F.S. 556.105(1)(a)). The day you call does not count.
  • Notice received outside business hours counts as received at the start of the next business day (556.105(1)(b)). This calculator treats a weekend/holiday call date the same way.
  • A ticket is valid 30 calendar days; the call day is not counted, and if the last day falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday it runs to the next business day (556.105(1)(c)).
  • Directional drilling and boring are excavation under Chapter 556 (Sunshine 811 FAQ). Each excavator, including each subcontractor, should pull its own ticket.
  • Verify positive responses before you dig (556.105(9)(c)).

Holidays used

Sunshine 811's closed days: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. When a fixed date falls on a weekend we use the observed weekday (e.g. Fri Jul 3, 2026). Sunshine 811 also says "state and national holidays" are excluded, so if you are near another holiday, confirm with 811.
